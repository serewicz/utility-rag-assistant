This is a plain-language summary written for this demo, not the official standard text.

CIP-004 covers security awareness, training, personnel risk assessments, and access management for people with authorized electronic or unescorted physical access to BES Cyber Systems.

Security awareness must be reinforced at least once each calendar quarter. Cyber security training must be completed before access is granted and at least once every 15 calendar months.

A personnel risk assessment, including a seven-year criminal history records check, is required before granting access. When a person is terminated, access must be removed within 24 hours of the termination action.
