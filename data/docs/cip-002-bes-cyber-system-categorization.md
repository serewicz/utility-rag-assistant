CIP-002 requires each Responsible Entity to identify and categorize its BES Cyber Systems as high, medium, or low impact. The categories depend on the asset's effect on the reliable operation of the Bulk Electric System.

A BES Cyber System at a control center that performs reliability functions for a large area is typically high impact. Generation and transmission substations meeting certain thresholds are medium impact. Remaining BES Cyber Systems are low impact.

The entity must review its identifications and categorizations at least once every 15 calendar months, and a CIP Senior Manager must approve them.
