# Flood Operations Notification Service

The LCRA Flood Operations Notification Service (FONS) alerts subscribers about flood operations along the [Highland Lakes](/water/dams-and-lakes/) and Lake Bastrop.

Subscribers are notified by email, text and/or a recorded phone call.

**How do I sign up?**

* Visit the [FONS registration page](https://getrave.com/login/extlcra).
* Review the instructions and FONS terms of use.
* Click on the green register button at the top right corner to create an account.

**When will my subscription begin?**  
You will be subscribed to FONS immediately after completing the registration process and responding to a confirmation email or text. The registration is through RAVE, a third-party provider that sends the alerts.

**How does FONS work?**

* It’s a free service that notifies subscribers when flood operations are expected to begin or have begun at dams along the Highland Lakes or Lake Bastrop.
* Subscribers can choose to receive alerts for any or all dams via text, email and/or phone calls.
* Notifications will not be sent for changes in operations or during routine gate testing and maintenance. For example, when a floodgate is later adjusted up or down, or when the gate is completely closed.
* Inks Dam does not have floodgates and is designed for floodwaters to pass over its spillway; FONS is activated for Inks Dam when water flows continuously over the spillway.

**How much notice will I receive before flood operations begin?**  
LCRA will send FONS notifications as soon as possible, but in some circumstances, there may be little or no advance notice of flood operations.

**Will FONS warn me of impending floods?**

* FONS is not a flood warning service. The service is a supplement to, and not a replacement for, vigilance on the part of residents and property owners.
* Anyone near the lakes or the lower Colorado River should monitor National Weather Service advisories, watches and warnings; local news media; and heed warnings from local emergency management officials when flooding poses a threat.

**How do I update my contact information or change my subscription?**  
Visit the [FONS registration page](https://getrave.com/login/extlcra) for instructions.
