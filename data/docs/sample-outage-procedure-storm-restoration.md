SAMPLE / FICTIONAL PROCEDURE. Written for this demo. Not a real utility procedure.

During storm restoration, crews restore service in this priority order: public safety hazards, critical facilities such as hospitals and water treatment plants, transmission and substations, main distribution feeders, then individual services.

Crews must check in with the storm staging area lead every four hours. Mutual assistance crews must receive a safety briefing before their first assignment.
