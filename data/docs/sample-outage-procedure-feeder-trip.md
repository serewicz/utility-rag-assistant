SAMPLE / FICTIONAL PROCEDURE. Written for this demo. Not a real utility procedure. Do not use in the field.

When a distribution feeder trips and fails to reclose, the field technician must first contact the Distribution Control Center and receive a switching order number before touching any equipment.

Patrol the feeder from the substation outward. Look for downed conductors, broken insulators, and vegetation contact. Treat every downed conductor as energized.

Before any work, apply personal protective grounds on both sides of the work zone and verify absence of voltage with a tested meter. Report the cause code to the control center when restoration is complete.
